LINUS PEARSON DEAD. Chairman of the Board of Elections Passed Away Today. WAS ILL SEVERAL WEEKS Linus E. Pearson, chairman of the Board of Election Commissioners, is dead. He passed" away this morning at hid residence, 17 Albion place, Charlestown, after an illness several weeks in dura tion. His death is said to havs been due to a general collapse, owing to overstrain upon the nerves and the body. The political fights of last fall were among the most fiercely contested in the his tory of the city, and the official duties were very wearing upon the chairman of the Elections Board. He was taken ill with pleurisy on the day following Thanksgiving, and was unable to return to his office in the Old Court House until Dec. 12, the day fol lowing ths municipal election. He was still a sick man, and the excitement and work of the week during which he stuck to his post caused him to again succumb. He ne-, er returned to his work again. He failed for weeks, and since Monday last his condition has been regarded as hopeless In the delirium which was the prelude of death, his mind almost con stantly dwelt upon his work as Elec tions Commissioner.
