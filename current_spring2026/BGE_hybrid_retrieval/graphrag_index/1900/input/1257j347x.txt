BIG BATTLE PROBABLY ON. IXJNDON, Jan 17.—The Exchange and Telegraph Company has received the following despatch, dated Tuesday, Jan. 16, from Pietermaritzburg: "There is no news from the front, but heavy firing was heard today in the direction of Frere. It is probable that General Buller Is engaging the enemy. A rumor is current here that a portion of the. British force is near Ladysmith." A despatch to the Daily Mail from Pietermaritzburg, dated Tuesday, says; There was very heavy firing to the north yesterday. I believe the Boers are contesting General Bullers passage of the Tugela.
